      How to assess
      • Judge the design you see: body proportions, face, build and overall presentation, from every side.
      • An anime or VRoid art style is not, by itself, a reason to record age-ambiguous. Many adult characters are drawn that way.
      • A declared or stated age is not enough on its own. The operator's declaration is shown for context; it is not evidence of the design.
      • If the design could reasonably be read as under 18, record age-ambiguous or appears-minor. If you cannot judge it from what is shown, record not-assessable.
      • A finding is permanent for these exact bytes. Only clearly-adult opens anything.
