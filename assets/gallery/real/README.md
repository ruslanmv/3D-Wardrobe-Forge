# Real-avatar gallery

Fifteen everyday and fashion looks on each of the four library avatars, **as
they arrive**: their own clothes, hair, skeleton and VRM 0.x rigs, fitted by
the native engine and rendered front 3/4 and front in the A-pose by the
Studio's viewer. Each image shows the source first. Its caption is the job's
own plan and fit report.

**60 of 60 looks pass their fit report.**

The avatars are CC0 (provenance in
[`assets/library/models.json`](../../library/models.json)). No declaration is
made for them, so these are looks that no adult gate applies to. The
materials verification set, including lingerie, sheer fabrics and layering on
the calibration mannequin, is in [`assets/gallery/`](../README.md). What
fitting a real body took is in [docs/FIT_QUALITY.md](../../../docs/FIT_QUALITY.md).
